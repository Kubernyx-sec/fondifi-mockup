FAQ

Common questions

Which property management platforms do you support?

AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi (Breeze and Voyager), Rent Manager, and QuickBooks for property management. Our team also works in Wave and Rentec Direct.

Do you handle trust account reconciliations?

Yes — full 3-way trust reconciliation is our core competency. We deliver a monthly audit-ready binder you can hand directly to your state auditor.

How fast can we get started?

Most clients are fully onboarded inside 5 business days. Catch-up engagements (cleanup) usually start the same week.

Where is your team based?

FONDiFi is headquartered in Lexington, Kentucky, with virtual teams that align to your time zone — coverage Mon–Sat 8:00–18:00.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. Operating engagements are month-to-month. Catch-up is a one-time flat fee.
