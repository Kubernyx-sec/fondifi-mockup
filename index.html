Do you actually know AppFolio and Buildium, or just generic bookkeeping?

Every FONDiFi bookkeeper is platform-certified before touching a client account. We work in AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi Breeze, Rent Manager, and QuickBooks every single day — not as a side specialty for tax-only firms. If a feature shipped in last week's release, we already know how it changes the close cycle.

How fast can you get my books current?

Standard catch-up engagements complete in 30 days. Trust account reconciliations are prioritized on day one — they're the highest-stakes piece, and we don't believe in dragging that work out.

If you're under audit notice from a state regulator, tell us in the first call. We re-sequence the engagement to put the audit packet first.

What's a 3-way trust reconciliation and why should I care?

A 3-way reconciliation matches three independent numbers: your bank balance, your trust ledger, and the sum of every individual owner ledger. They must all equal each other to the penny.

Most state real-estate boards require it for license renewal. If yours doesn't yet, it will. We run it every month and sign it.

Where is your team based?

HQ is Lexington, Kentucky. Every account is supervised by a US-based lead — the person who signs your reconciliation lives and works in the United States. We staff our pods globally to keep pricing fair, but accountability lives here.

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?

A VA is a person you have to train, supervise, and replace when they leave. We're a back-office function: a pod of trained operators, supervised by a named lead, with documented standards. If someone on your pod leaves, your close calendar doesn't slip.

Can you take over from my current bookkeeper without breaking continuity?

Yes. We do a "clean handoff" engagement: we receive the existing files, run a trust audit on the most recent close, document any open items, and ship the first full close 30 days later. Your residents and owners see no interruption.
